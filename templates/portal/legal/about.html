About {{ site_settings.site_name }}

{{ site_settings.site_name }} is a dedicated, real-time automated education news and information aggregation portal designed to deliver verified, fast, and accessible updates on government examinations, recruitments, results, admit cards, and admissions.

Our Mission

Our objective is to bridge the information gap between official government education authorities and students/aspirants across the country. Government notifications are frequently buried within complex portals or posted in fragmented formats. We automatically track, parse, and structure these public announcements into clear, digestible, and actionable formats.

How We Aggregate Information

We use high-precision automated systems that continuously monitor public feeds and official circulars from major recruiting commissions, testing agencies, and boards (including SSC, UPSC, NTA, RRB, CBSE, and State Boards).

  • Strict Fact-Grounding: We only publish verified information extracted directly from official notifications. If information is not specified in the notice, it is explicitly declared as unavailable rather than guessed.
  • Direct Link Transparency: Every article includes direct, verifiable links to the original government portal and the official PDF notification.
  • Real-Time Updates: If an authority amends a notice or extends an application deadline, our system updates the article and logs the version history.

Independent Information Portal

{{ site_settings.site_name }} is an independent public information utility and is not affiliated, associated, authorized, endorsed by, or in any way officially connected with any government ministry, commission, or agency.
